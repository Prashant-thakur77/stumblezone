# Making it winnable — the idea, not the logistics (2026-09-08)

Judges test in the mobile app, mostly alone, a few minutes each, across dozens of entries. What
they remember is one sentence, one moment, and whether anything broke. This plan fixes the three
things that would cost us against a strong field.

## 1. The sentence (originality, discovery)

Today the README opens with "Fall Guys-style". A judge reads "clone". The original part is the
premise: **a game show that runs itself on the clock, where the crowd plays too, in a village you
can walk.** Rewrite the first paragraph of README, `scene.json`'s description and the BUIDL
one-liner around that; keep the genre reference as a footnote, not a headline.

## 2. A round only Decentraland can host — Copycat (creativity, social, mobile-first)

The scene can read every avatar's emote events (`AvatarEmoteCommand`). So: the jumbotron shows a
sequence of poses - DANCE > CLAP > WAVE - and every player has to perform it, in order, in time.
Six big pose buttons on the phone; the emote wheel works too. Wrong or late costs a heart, the
sequence grows each wave, and from the ledge it is a synchronised dance-off. Nothing in it can
hurt you physically; it is the show's breather round, and the one that looks like Decentraland.

- `src/lib/copycat.ts`: poses, seeded sequences (2,3,4,5 long), the show/perform schedule, the
  judge. Tests: sequences never repeat a pose twice in a row, timing covers the round, judging.
- `src/arena/rounds/copycat.ts`: the disc stage, the banner, `AvatarEmoteCommand.onChange` on
  the local player, `triggerEmote` from the HUD buttons, `loseLife` on a miss.
- Round id 6, in the pool at difficulty 2. HUD: a pose row shown only during the perform window.

## 3. An opponent who is always there — Beat the House (retention for a solo judge)

Every round gets a house time. Survive past it (or finish under it in Tip Toe) and the card says
**BEAT THE HOUSE +1**, alone or not. A judge at 3 am has something to beat that is not their own
best from thirty seconds ago.

## 4. The first thirty seconds — a one-time HOW TO PLAY card (onboarding, usability)

Three lines and a GOT IT button, once per session, over the lobby. Sam's bubble stays for the
details; this is for the judge who reads nothing else.

## Not doing

Cross-session persistence (the gated Multiplayer Server spike) - three days out, it is the one
change that can take the World down. Fake players - a judge would see through it in a minute.

## Status

All four shipped 2026-09-08: pitch rewritten (README, scene.json, SUBMISSION), Copycat (round id 6,
coach avatar, pose buttons + emote wheel, 133 tests), Beat the House (par per round, +1 x stakes),
the welcome card (hides the intro card and splash while up). Smoke run: 1,207 entities, no errors.
