# Fun cycle 1 — five changes that make a round more fun to be in (2026-09-08)

Places are done. This cycle is about moments: what happens to *you* in a round, and what a
spectator gets to do about it.

| # | Change | Why it is fun | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Intro flyover** — six-second crane shot over the arena during the round card | You see the board you are about to play, like a TV show's establishing shot | `camera.ts` `flyover()`, scheduler intro |
| B | **Pick a winner** — spectators tap a name; if it qualifies +1, if it wins outright +2 | Being out now has a stake in who is in | `lib/bet.ts`, HUD buttons on the ledge |
| C | **GG** — one button on the results card sends "GG" to your rival; they see a toast | The rivalry line becomes a conversation | `sync.ts` `gg`, HUD |
| D | **Power-ups** — SHIELD (absorbs one heart) and BOOST (faster, higher for 8 s) appear at 20 s and 50 s on clock-driven rounds; everyone can take each once, nothing is synced | A micro-goal near the danger, and a comeback tool | `lib/powerups.ts`, `systems/powerups.ts`, `spectator.ts` shield |
| E | **Leader call-outs** — "Alice leads the show with 5" at every round start; "FINALE: Alice vs the field" | Names the person to beat | scheduler `beginSlot` |

Rules: no new sync except `gg`; power-ups are per-player and seeded, so fair without a server;
nothing here touches a player who is not in the round. Tests: bet resolution, power-up timing and
placement, collect-once.

## Cycles 2 and 3 (same day)

**Cycle 2 — juice:** Jump Bar beams reverse at 70 s · Spotlight blackout at 60 s, lights come back
elsewhere · FINAL TWO call-out with a pink jumbotron · ON FIRE at a three-streak, for everyone ·
BOO next to CHEER · last-five-seconds numerals · crowd bed volume follows the hype meter.

**Cycle 3 — twists inside rounds:** Perfect Match SWITCH (waves 3+, half the time, 1.8 s before
the judge, four safe tiles guaranteed for the new colour) · Tip Toe gold tile (a real tile past
the first row, +1 once) · Hex-Drop crumble (a seeded third of the top deck at 60 s) · PAST YOUR
BEST mid-round · MVP at results.

All three cycles: 126 tests, no new sync beyond `gg`, every twist derived from the seed and the
clock so every client sees the same thing.
